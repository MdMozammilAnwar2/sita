01 / A little about me

Leadership is a practice. Growth is a journey.

Across a career spanning consulting, operations, learning and development, and leadership coaching, I’ve been drawn to one enduring question: how do we help people do their best work and become the leaders they’re capable of being?

My experience includes a decade-long chapter at Deloitte, leadership roles with Toastmasters International, and building uExcelerate — a professional development platform working to make coaching accessible across organizations. I bring together strategic thinking, thoughtful facilitation, and a deep interest in human behaviour to help individuals and teams navigate change with greater clarity.

I’m passionate about skill development for students and professionals, and have contributed to learning initiatives across colleges, corporate environments, and government institutions.
